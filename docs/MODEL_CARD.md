# Model Card — SpendIQ Demo

## Intended use
Educational demonstration of a forecasting pipeline, RFM segmentation, and campaign response classification.

## Out of scope
Credit underwriting, lending decisions, fraud decisions, pricing, eligibility decisions, real customer targeting, or automated decisions affecting people.

## Data
Synthetic transactions generated locally by `src/data.py`. No production data is used.

## Evaluation
- **Forecasting**: rolling-origin (one-step-ahead) backtest over the final 12 of 36 months. Random forest and XGBoost
  (month-over-month-ratio features), Holt-Winters ETS and ARIMA(1,1,1) on log spend are compared with a last-month
  baseline and a seasonal-naive (t-12) baseline using MAE, RMSE and MAPE. Every backtest prediction is made by a model trained only on earlier months. The best model by MAE
  is reported even when it is a baseline.
- **Campaign response**: transaction-level classifier with category, channel, region, amount, exposure, calendar
  features and customer-history features computed from strictly earlier transactions. Chronological 80/20 split by
  timestamp. Reports precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC (with an exposure-only reference), lift by score decile,
  confusion matrix and permutation importance on the test set.

## Limitations
- The dataset is synthetic and intentionally simplified.
- 36 monthly points give only a 12-month backtest; metrics are unstable and seasonality is generated by construction.
- The campaign model is transaction-level: customers appear in both train and test periods, and prior responses are assumed to be observed immediately.
- Campaign labels are simulated and do not demonstrate causal lift.
- Model performance on synthetic data cannot be generalized to real financial services.
- Fairness, privacy, drift, and security evaluation are not comprehensive.

## Human oversight
Any real-world application would require domain review, legal/compliance review, validated data controls, independent testing, monitoring, and documented approval.

## Merchant intelligence
- **Cleaning**: ordered regular-expression rules map noisy descriptors to fictional merchants. Coverage, precision
  and category agreement are measured against the generator's true merchant (unavailable on real data).
- **Merchant forecast**: pooled XGBoost on merchant-month ratio features, one-step-ahead, refit before each of the
  last 6 months, compared with last-month and seasonal-naive on WAPE. Unmatched descriptors are excluded.
- Because model selection uses the same backtest that reports its error, the displayed error is mildly optimistic.
